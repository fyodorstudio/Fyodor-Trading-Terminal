S&P and Nasdaq Close Lower S&P and Nasdaq Close Lower 2026-11-02 19:33 By Joana Taborda 1 min. read US stocks kicked off the November month mostly lower, with the S&P 500 falling 0.5% and the Nasdaq losing 1.1%, while the Dow Jones rose 214 points. Losses in energy and technology shares weighed on the broader market. Investors remained cautious ahead of the upcoming midterm elections and key labour market data due later this week. Meanwhile, oil prices declined amid growing hopes that the US and Iran could resume peace talks. Apple shares fell 1.8% ahead of the company's earnings release after the closing bell. Other major technology stocks also came under pressure, with Microsoft down 2.8%, Micron Technology tumbling 5.8% and AMD sliding 7.5%. ExxonMobil also dropped 4%. Oracle plunged 8.8% after announcing a $10 billion share offering alongside plans to raise a similar amount through a debt sale. In contrast, consumer discretionary, materials and financials stocks outperformed. Amazon climbed 2.2%, Tesla gained 1%, while Walmart advanced 3.4%.
