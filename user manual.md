# What to tick in Inspector and Alert

## Trade candidates

> I only consider trades around selected major **policy, inflation or labour/wage
> releases**. Before entering, I need a meaningful surprise, a clear explanation
> for the pair's direction, and defined invalidation and risk.

Choose major releases within those families; every speech or minor employment
statistic need not become a candidate. Passing on other opportunities is fine.
The shortlist narrows your attention; your entry and exit rules still need testing.

| Check before considering an entry | What to establish |
|---|---|
| Expectations | What was already expected or priced in? Keep broker forecasts and identified web surveys separately attributed. |
| Interpretation | Does the complete release—including guidance, components and revisions—change the expected future rate path? |
| Pair and entry | Does the other currency's backdrop support the view? Is price sustaining the reaction? Where is the trade invalidated, and what loss is acceptable? |

Mixed components, an already-priced outcome or a quick reversal can leave no
clear edge. A forecast beat or large news candle alone is insufficient.

Leave the remaining research to the agent. Growth, energy, financial conditions
and exchange-rate developments still belong in the backdrop and can outweigh
the release being watched. Other checked families in the tables provide context.
[Fed policy considerations](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/principles-for-the-conduct-of-monetary-policy.htm),
[SNB policy and exchange rates](https://www.snb.ch/en/the-snb/mandates-goals/monetary-policy/strategy)

## Filter setup

Start with your pair's two currencies. Each table runs from highest to lowest
**observation priority**; ★ means tick these first. Add other rows as you learn.
This is a practical starting order: promote a lower row when the bank or backdrop
makes it the main concern. Priority does not predict the size of a move.

In **Inspector → Filters**, tick the individual family names below, then **Save**.
Search the name to find it; ticking a whole category selects its other families too.
In **Alert → Filters**, choose your currencies, tick the same ★ families and **Save**.
Alert has its own selection; Inspector changes do not update it.

Open a listed upcoming release's **Research** entry to prepare a possible setup.
Ask the agent for backdrop, expectations and stronger/weaker scenarios using the
trade-candidate checks above.

## USD

| Priority | Tick this family in Inspector / Alert | Upcoming release to review → focus |
|---|---|---|
| ★ 1 | US FOMC | Decision, press conference, projections when published → rate path versus pricing; a hold can strengthen or weaken USD. |
| ★ 2 | US CPI / core CPI | CPI → monthly core/headline surprise and persistent inflation. |
| ★ 3 | US Jobs report / NFP | Jobs report → payrolls **plus revisions, unemployment and earnings** together. |
| ★ 4 | US PCE inflation | PCE → core monthly persistence; what CPI/PPI already suggested. |
| 5 | US Retail Sales | Retail sales → underlying spending and revisions. |
| 6 | US ISM Manufacturing / Services | ISM → services, prices and employment components. |
| 7 | US Jobless Claims | Weekly claims → sustained change, especially when labour is the main concern. |
| 8 | US GDP | First estimate/material revisions → growth composition and new information. |
| 9 | US PPI | PPI → components relevant to consumer/PCE inflation. |
| 10 | Fed Chair speeches / testimony; FOMC minutes | Policy remarks/minutes → new guidance; check what is already outdated. |

Inflation and employment are policy anchors; the Fed's inflation goal uses PCE.
[Federal Reserve](https://www.federalreserve.gov/faqs/what-economic-goals-does-federal-reserve-seek-to-achieve-through-monetary-policy.htm)

## The smallest useful EURUSD setup

Tick **US FOMC**, **US CPI / core CPI**, **US Jobs report / NFP**, **US PCE inflation**
and **ECB policy**, **Euro-area inflation**, **Euro-area PMI · France / Germany / Euro area**.
Start Alert with those same families. Add rows when they answer a specific question.
Routine speeches, housing, trade, fiscal and miscellaneous releases can wait in
your initial viewing shortlist. Research exports still preserve every stored
release for the pair and dates, regardless of filters.

For a highlighted Alert entry, ask:

> Read AGENTS.md. Prepare this release for EURUSD: what is expected, what is the
> backdrop, which outcomes would support each currency, and what would invalidate
> the view? After release, check the actual package and whether price sustained or
> reversed its first move.

For historical work, use **Research date range**, name the report and add notes.
**Prepare research** groups the brief and complete evidence under
`Market Analyst Workflow/report/<name>-<timestamp>/`. Copy the brief path and ask
the agent to explain the changing backdrop. It can fetch calendar and OHLC using
[AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md#dataset-commands). Broker forecasts and web surveys stay
separately attributed; different providers/methods can produce different numbers.
