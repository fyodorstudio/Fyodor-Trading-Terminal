# One Minute Before Release

Use this only for an imminent named release/countdown in the active chat.
Read [research rules](../../../docs/RESEARCH-RULES.md). Reuse prepared sources,
attributed expectations, policy context, affected currencies and contrary facts.
Do not start an unrelated eight-currency sweep near publication.

Verify the exact publication instant against an official schedule. Decision,
statement, projections and press conference may be separate material stages.
Date-only or unresolved conflicting timing cannot support a precise countdown.
Read the available clock before announcing a countdown; show Jakarta AM/PM.
Default stopping bound: five minutes after the targeted publication. If already
past it, make a bounded current check and disclose lateness; never pretend to
have observed the release live. Respect changed scope, deadline or cancellation.

Use short interruptible waits, at most 30 seconds, and reread the clock after
each wait or retrieval. Give useful progress at least each minute. At release,
check the prepared official endpoint and exact local occurrence if relevant.
While decisive actuals are missing within the bound, make focused sequential
checks roughly 5–10 seconds apart, backing off on failures. Stop at the bound
and name unavailable fields; do not extend into background monitoring.

Verify period, units, revisions, definitions and material simultaneous releases.
Forecast/previous values, elapsed release time and a new capture clock do not
prove fresh actuals. Distinguish operation start/close from result publication.
Interpret the package against expectations and policy concerns, including
material opposition and unresolved guidance. Never count components as votes.

Deliver the first supported currency implication promptly with actuals versus
attributed expectations, causal reason, source links and material remaining gaps.
A currency implication alone does not establish a preferred cross or trade.
Compare both sides only when requested. Current price feedback requires an
explicit price-aware request and follows the first fundamental answer.

Unless chat-only/no-save, save the affected currency news through
[Currency News](../../../docs/CURRENCY-NEWS.md) after delivery, preserving each
source's original publication time and any unresolved coverage. Verify read-back
and the running panel API. Do not revive Thesis, checkpoint renewal, outcome
scoring or alarm mutations. Optional dataset commands live in
[AGENT-RESEARCH.md](../../../docs/AGENT-RESEARCH.md#dataset-commands).
No background AI invocation follows.
