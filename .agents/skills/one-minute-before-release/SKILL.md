# One Minute Before Release

Use Assess Trade Now's [research procedure](../assess-trade-now/SKILL.md).
A release invocation refreshes the affected live Thesis mechanisms under standing
save permission, unless read-only/chat-only/no-save. After the first supported
verdict, save verified developments and a focused assessment through
[Thesis research](../assess-trade-now/references/thesis-research.md); preserve other
currencies. Do not start an unrelated all-eight investigation or duplicate daily
snapshots. Record offsets and subsequent guidance, not only the first actual.

Original evidence dates, Last Assessed and the next review deadline are separate.
An unresolved decisive stage stays an explicit gap. No alarm or passing time
refreshes a verdict. The [Thesis contract](../../../docs/THESIS.md) owns saves and
read-back; never delay the first sourced decision for persistence/bookkeeping.
The left **Fundamentals** panel is the user's primary view. After the first
supported verdict, update its affected ledger assessments and complete the
parent's [left-panel audit](../assess-trade-now/references/thesis-research.md#left-panel-freshness-audit),
preserving other currencies and old evidence clocks. A chat verdict or successful
save alone does not establish that the panel has a valid refreshed summary.
Significant reminders use the parent's alert procedure. This skill performs a
bounded active-chat wait, not background monitoring, trading or automatic AI
invocation. A commodity release needs its own material evidence and identity.

## Reuse preparation

Resolve the requested occurrence, broker, reference period and pair from the
prompt and prior assessment; an invocation plus event/date is sufficient when
that context resolves the scope. Inherit the Jakarta AM/PM-only presentation
default; use UTC internally, without displaying additional timezones.
Apply later scope changes immediately: "just the USD bias" stops pair selection
and uses Assess Trade Now's concise currency-only response.
Carry forward its sources, policy concerns,
separately attributed forecasts, conditional scenarios, counterevidence,
extreme-move risks and the prepared at-least-seven-day fundamental exit/
reassessment watchlist. Keep their as-of times and refresh schedule changes.
Use the parent's [efficient refreshes](../assess-trade-now/references/thesis-research.md#efficient-refreshes)
to reuse shared evidence and known routes without repeating unchanged research.
Retain eight-currency context if available, but compare all seven counterparts
only when the user has requested that scope or best-event-pair selection;
otherwise assess the requested pair or candidate list. A subsequently narrowed
candidate list takes precedence. Remember the conditional shortlist and why
each counterpart was selected; fresh actuals can change the winner. Do not repeat
comprehensive research near publication.
Reuse prepared LOOK HERE NEXT candidates and refresh exceptional beneficiary or
haven changes. The first release verdict must not wait for a new eight-currency
sweep, unrelated candidate receipt or price check. Complete deferred preparation
within the existing deadline and disclose remaining coverage precisely.

Carry forward the prepared official endpoint, fallback and any focused local query,
minimum decisive fields and simultaneous-release checks. Before publication,
resolve decisive retrieval/expectation gaps and refresh exceptional news. Do not
turn a conditional shortlist into a fixed numeric score or preselect its winner.
Identify which simultaneous releases for the selected currencies could materially
change the pair view, and retain their focused queries alongside the named event.
They form part of the minimum decisive package when material, even if they are
outside the named Highlight's stored series IDs.

If preparation is missing or stale, refresh only decisive gaps. Say when the
coverage cannot be completed before publication; do not claim a full eight-
currency review from a thirty-second preparation. An unsupported counterpart
cannot be selected merely because another currency's release is clear.
In a fresh chat, invoke Assess Trade Now's shared preparation directly; it
supplies its scoped pin/icon authorization without a second user request. Read
the clock first, verify the official release route and attributable expectations,
then check counterparts. If seconds remain, prioritize fresh actuals and the
first sourced verdict; expand the comparison within the declared deadline.
Call an incompletely compared choice the leading verified candidate, and name
the specific remaining coverage once. Keep the direct directional thesis.

When an auditable coverage receipt is useful, the optional discovery helper can
record opened evidence and actual failures with its release/deadline bound. A
partial receipt is not complete coverage. Do not delay actuals, fabricate route
attempts or extend the wait for bookkeeping.

## Verify time and wait

- Verify the exact publication instant against the official schedule, reusing
  already verified evidence when still applicable. Separate decision, statement,
  projections and press conference stages. Show the target in Jakarta AM/PM;
  retain the verified UTC instant internally.
  A date-only or unresolved conflicting schedule cannot support a precise timer.
- Read the available clock tool's current UTC time and calculate remaining time
  from that verified instant, rather than reusing an old countdown. Resolve any
  inconsistent client/runtime clocks before claiming a live countdown. If clock
  or wait tools are unavailable, explain the limitation; do not claim to be waiting.
- Read the clock before announcing a countdown or coverage deadline. Announce
  the target, scope, preparation gaps and stopping deadline. Default to
  five minutes after the targeted publication unless the user specifies another
  bound. If invoked after release, check actuals immediately; do not wait again
  for a past instant. If already beyond the deadline, make one current check and
  report the result without pretending to have observed publication live. State
  the lateness and perform one bounded retrieval pass with a suitable fallback;
  do not announce an expired deadline as future coverage or begin another wait.
- Use available short, interruptible waits (such as clock.sleep), at most 30
  seconds each, capped by time remaining to the next check or deadline. Re-read
  the clock after waits and tool calls; new user input may end a wait early.
  Use useful commentary about changes or missing actuals at least every minute,
  without narrating unchanged polls. Stop on cancellation or changed scope.
- At publication, retrieve the prepared official release route. For a named
  Highlight, also query its exact --summary; other local readings are optional.
  Read-only CLI queries do not refresh the publisher. The storage collector waits
  two seconds between cycles,
  but broker/publisher arrival can lag; never promise numbers from that interval.
  While decisive actuals remain missing within the active bound, make focused
  sequential checks roughly 5–10 seconds apart, allowing for command duration
  and backing off on failures.
  Do not busy-loop, query the full inventory or repeatedly reload instructions.

For a requested operation window **and subsequent results**, distinguish start,
close and result publication. Start is not a result release. Cover through five
minutes after the verified close by default, announce that bound and stop there
if results have not arrived. Keep the exact result minute unknown until verified;
this does not authorize waiting for other operations or ongoing monitoring.

Record available timing checkpoints in the active conversation: scheduled release,
first fresh decisive-field observation, sufficient package for interpretation,
and first-verdict send attempt. Use clock/tool timestamps already available;
do not delay the verdict just to collect timing. Distinguish source/provider
timestamps from our observation time and attempted send from the user's receipt.
Missing checkpoints stay unknown. A first successful retrieval does not establish
when data first became public or how long reasoning alone took. Summarize measured
intervals briefly with the final evidence, not on every poll; do not persist
telemetry or research without authorization.

## Fresh actuals, then interpret

Preparation includes the parent's [causal weighting](../assess-trade-now/references/fundamental-assessment.md#causal-weighting):
carry the bank's current policy concerns and active major evidence into publication.
A secondary release can have a directional impact without overturning the overall
currency verdict. Missing preparation is a focused research task, not permission
to mechanically replace the policy backdrop with the latest headline.

Check the exact period, series definitions, units, actuals, revisions and decisive
components against the prepared expectations. A new capture timestamp, elapsed
release time, unchanged value or empty field is not proof of publication; equally,
a genuinely unchanged actual can be valid if current-release evidence confirms
it. Never substitute forecast, previous value or the prior release for today's
actual. Check the release package as a whole, not as a count of bullish fields.
Before the first combined pair verdict, read the prepared material simultaneous
releases and revisions as well as the named release. An in-line inflation reading,
for example, does not establish that a simultaneous trade/activity release was
neutral. If a decisive companion is still missing, immediately report the verified
named-release interpretation and the pending check; withhold a completed combined
pair verdict until sufficient evidence arrives or the deadline is reached. Do not
delay for nondecisive details or begin another broad calendar scan.
For policy releases, apply Assess Trade Now's expectation-relative rate-path
check: the headline hike/cut is insufficient when guidance, projections or a
pending decisive stage could overturn it. Retrieve the prepared decisive path/
guidance fields before a pair verdict; do not default to "hike = buy" or "cut =
sell". If only the rate is confirmed, distinguish that fact and the conditional
backdrop lean from a verified full-package interpretation.

Retrieve the prepared official release endpoint and any useful focused local
readings in parallel where possible. Consume completed checks as they become
available; a slow nondecisive request must not hold up a supported first verdict. Avoid general
web discovery when a prepared direct route works. If the official page fails,
try one suitable alternative:
fresh broker readings or accessible reliable reporting of the exact release.
Attribute any provisional broker-only assessment and missing official confirmation.
If web extraction fails, one direct official HTTP text/CSV retrieval may be the
fallback; inspect its period/content rather than treating a successful status or
search snippet as confirmation. Avoid broad discovery and repeated failed URLs
when a focused official route or the complete verified broker package suffices.
Search snippets alone do not confirm a decisive fact. Material disagreement or
missing decisive fields prevents a confirmed event-specific interpretation;
identify the unresolved issue separately from any supported overall bias.
Do not wait for a slow website if sufficiently verified current evidence already
supports an explicitly attributed interpretation. Do not delay for nondecisive
details or repeatedly search unchanged pages.

Refresh intervening news since preparation, especially simultaneous releases,
changed guidance, intervention and other exceptional risks for the selected pair.
Use a focused check, not a second full macro investigation. Interpret how the
surprise changes the expected policy path; an in-line release need not provide
a new edge. Before-release hypotheses may be confirmed, weakened or overturned.
The first release verdict is fundamental-first. A subsequent entry-focused
assessment uses Assess Trade Now's manual technical workflow; a bounded price
check needs an explicit price-aware request and no-candles overrides remain binding. Never delay actuals, decisive
companions, publication checks or the first verdict to inspect price. Full
historical reaction calculations still use Review Historical Research.

Apply Assess Trade Now's
[continuation/retracement check](../assess-trade-now/references/fundamental-assessment.md#continuation-retracement-or-a-changed-thesis)
to the evidence available now. The release's first verdict is not a forecast of
the entire session: later guidance or a separate release can warrant an opposite
short-term bias without erasing the initial facts. Separate an active retracement
thesis supported by new information from a hypothetical reversal risk. Do not
delay a supported first verdict to await speculative later developments.

Apply the parent's [release and retention check](../assess-trade-now/references/fundamental-assessment.md#release-and-retention-check)
before retaining direction after contrary guidance. Label which stages the first
verdict covers. Check material later remarks already available within the active
deadline; do not finish with an opening-stage verdict presented as a completed
conference review. A later scheduled stage outside the bound is a separate
checkpoint, not permission to extend the wait. If decisive Q&A remains unavailable,
state that gap and withhold the unsupported fresh full-stage conclusion; a final
official transcript is not required when credible reporting verifies the remarks.
Use **Provisional — statement only; conference pending** when that is the verified
information set, and persist its explicit review status and stage note. A pending
stage cannot be hidden only in an Upcoming list or tooltip. At a later invocation,
resolve that stage before renewing the session call; a failed checkpoint-only
retrieval saves a gap without freshening the old direction clock. Apply the
parent's decision check: an expected hold, in-line core print or strong output
alone does not establish a more restrictive forward path.

For speeches, panels and Q&A, verify the actual delivery/publication format,
announced topic and any quiet-period or explicit policy-discussion restriction.
"No prepared text" or an empty transcript page does not mean no policy remarks
were made. If the live remarks cannot be verified, preserve any supported
backdrop lean but explicitly withhold a fresh event-specific verdict. An event's
scheduled start is not proof the decisive remarks have been delivered.
When a verified report says the speaker will not discuss current policy, report
that promptly and stop waiting for that promised rate signal. Attribute reported
remarks and distinguish them from an official transcript. Newly locating a report
does not mean its contents were newly published during this invocation.
If asked where to watch, provide the organiser's exact event/day stream and
Jakarta schedule. A listed livestream does not establish active playback; say
whether broadcasting was verified. A watch-link follow-up alone starts no wait.

## Respond and stop

Use two deliveries in the same active turn. As soon as the decisive package
supports an interpretation, send a short first verdict in commentary: the requested
pair BUY/SELL or currency STRENGTHENING BIAS / WEAKENING BIAS, one causal
sentence and the decisive actuals versus attributed expectations, with a direct
source. Follow Assess Trade Now's direction rule, including qualitative STRONG
BUY/SELL only when supported; no MIXED/neutral fallback or invented probability.
The task is the verified release's potential currency impact and intensity, not
proof of realised flows or an execution-ready entry. Missing live pricing or known
opposition does not justify withholding that impact or returning Review needed.
Apply Assess Trade Now's catalyst-dominance rule;
routine counterevidence does not automatically
weaken the verdict. Briefly identify a material unresolved fact when necessary.
Give one concrete invalidation. State RETAINED or REVERSED against a prepared
comparable verdict; if an opposite mechanism dominates, state that direction
promptly rather than leaving it as recovery risk. A decisive unknown may require
withdrawing the earlier call, separately from the verified release facts.
Do not wait to format the final answer or repeat unchanged flashes on every poll.
If decisive fields conflict or are missing, identify the gap without inventing a
confirmed release result; a supported existing thesis can still be stated.

Then use Assess Trade Now's [compact output](../assess-trade-now/SKILL.md#output-format):
assessment date/as-of time and horizon, a direct thesis, a separate **NEAR-TERM & UPCOMING EVENTS** section, then **DATED FUNDAMENTAL
BACKDROP**: a compact category-by-currency comparison of monetary policy
and rate projections, inflation, labor/wages, growth/activity and other drivers,
with dated evidence, ages and implications. Reuse the prepared table and refresh
the released package and intervening news; do not rebuild everything near release.
Surface supported LOOK HERE NEXT candidates from preparation near the top.
Recent bullets go newest first; upcoming events go earliest first using the
prepared seven-day scan. Integrate material reversal triggers and confirmation
gaps briefly. Use only the compact backdrop table by
default; no mandatory entry-date declarations or confidence/entry/risk checklists.

In requested best-pair mode, give one leading pair and at most a brief useful
alternative, using the prepared counterpart evidence. User technicals do not
preselect the pair or strengthen the fundamental evidence. State whether actuals
are verified or provisional; a first verdict is not a promise of an immediate fill.
Follow the parent's manual technical workflow: default to fundamental-only output,
including on a price-alert follow-up. Give BUY/SELL, both currency mechanisms,
horizon, invalidation and the next material checkpoint. Consider a non-USD cross
only when prepared/refreshed evidence supports it more clearly; never delay the
release verdict to build a new cross comparison. Call a partially compared choice
the leading verified candidate. Price/candle checks need an explicit request.

The final response must stand alone. Reuse prepared evidence and complete only
material unresolved checks within the original deadline; do not start another
full research pass or an unrequested later monitoring stage. If fresh evidence
changes the first verdict, issue a clearly labelled correction promptly before
the final assessment. Identify the additional/corrected information, its source and
whether it changes the direction or conviction; distinguish a later release
from an already-published simultaneous result discovered late. Include available
timing checkpoints/intervals compactly and label them as our observations, not
proof of when data first became public.
The first-verdict latency is separate from completion of the dated assessment. Neither
is a promise of a response within a fixed number of seconds after publication.

Before stopping, name any nearer verified policy stage or speaker that could
challenge the call, including overnight events, rather than defaulting to the
next major data release. State that this verdict is as of the last check and
that monitoring ends here; a later reassessment requires another invocation
unless the user has explicitly requested a longer bounded wait. A suggested
return time is not an alarm or continuing coverage.

If decisive evidence remains unavailable at the deadline, report exactly what is
missing/conflicting, last check time and what would allow reassessment. Preserve
a supported overall directional bias while stating that the event adds no verified
update. Only a decisive gap in the overall thesis permits "Bias cannot be
established"; do not use that phrase or staying flat as routine substitutes for
weighing the evidence. Finish the active wait; do not silently continue monitoring.
Do not wait for a later policy stage unless it is the requested target or necessary for
the verdict within the agreed bound; name pending stages in the separate Upcoming section.

Apply the standing focused-save workflow above during a live invocation. An
Alert firing without an invocation performs no research or write. Follow the
[Thesis workflow](../../../docs/THESIS.md): append the sourced verdict with its
real as-of time and preserve prior versions. A release link is optional metadata,
not a pending row to fill. Confirm the save or disclose failure; do not backdate
or modify pins/raw data. Saving does not extend the wait or start monitoring.

An Assess Trade Now handoff retains its standing material-event pin/icon
permission and immediate-discovery context; do not repeat the whole scan before
a first supported release verdict. Focused live Thesis updates use the standing
save permission above; full Highlight journals retain their separate save rule.
No entry price, stop, sizing, profit forecast, candle confirmation, order,
automatic archiving or ongoing monitoring is implied.
Creating this skill does not start a timer or create an automation.
