# Standalone currency verdicts and assessment freshness

The user's workflow is fundamental research in Age | CUR | VERDICT | Checkpoint,
with their own technical analysis and manual price alerts. The panel is a
standalone view of each currency's current fundamental pressure. Never populate
it by copying a currency's leg from a USD pair or another named cross.

## Direction, intensity and horizon

Research the currency's own policy path relative to prior expectations, inflation,
labour/wages, activity, fiscal/trade conditions and material global exposure.
Include active funding, commodity, risk and haven channels where verified.
Give the potential strengthening/weakening impact, its intensity and causal channel.
Explain the dominant remaining pressure and the strongest actual opposition.
Do not infer standalone strength from EUR/USD, USD/JPY or one other quoted pair.
An old policy decision, absolute yield gap or an already-expected hike alone is
not an active directional thesis. Reported flow explanations need attribution;
unobserved investor intentions remain inference.

For a retained verdict, apply the [release and retention check](fundamental-assessment.md#release-and-retention-check).
Explain why the remaining mechanism still dominates new opposition over this
currency's horizon. A weaker bullish case does not by itself prove weakening.
Keep a longer-term backdrop distinct from immediate pressure and do not present a
partial release review as a completed session view.

Save direction.basis = standalone, verdict = POTENTIAL STRENGTHENING or
POTENTIAL WEAKENING, intensity = strong / moderate / slight and a concise horizon
covering the current assessment through its next material review. Intensity is
qualitative force and breadth of the supported mechanism, not confidence,
probability, return, candle size or a numeric currency score:

- Strong: an active material catalyst or sustained pressure clearly dominates
  the verified opposition over this horizon.
- Moderate: a clear active mechanism dominates with meaningful offsets.
- Slight: the balance has a supported but limited directional advantage, with
  substantial offsets or modest incremental pressure.

These labels express potential causal impact, not proof that investors have
already acted. A material catalyst can merit Strong despite routine opposition;
do not automatically compress every assessment to Slight because markets are
uncertain. Explain an event's intensity in its development implication separately
from the saved currency assessment when other active events change the net view.
Use the existing implication/reason fields; no extra verdict store is needed.

Do not force a Strong label, count releases as votes or choose direction to
match a later chart. Any decisive unresolved fact still needs honest disclosure.
Use the same qualitative scale across all eight currencies. Its purpose includes
comparing currencies pointing in the same direction, using fresh assessments
with overlapping horizons, through the earlier material checkpoint:

- Both strengthening: favour buying the currency with stronger supported pressure.
- Both weakening: favour buying the currency with weaker weakening pressure.
- Base strengthening and quote weakening favour BUY; the reverse favours SELL.
- Equal grades are the start of a comparison, not its conclusion. Compare the
  mechanisms, fresh changes and material opposition over the same horizon. Separate
  grades when the causal difference warrants it. Within one grade, give a supported
  qualitative relative preference and reason when the evidence distinguishes the
  currencies; do not invent a numerical difference or change grades to fit a chart.

For example, slight EUR weakening with moderate JPY weakening can support EUR/JPY
BUY. Moderate AUD strengthening with slight CAD strengthening can support AUD/CAD
BUY. These are conditional illustrations, not standing currency assignments.
Weigh material pair-specific opposition before calling an entry. Standalone
pressure is not a promise of appreciation against all seven currencies, a pip
forecast or proof of institutional positioning. A previous USD comparison may
change independently of this current broad pressure ranking.

A broad assessment includes a concise comparison of relevant same-direction
currencies, rather than eight independent labels with no explanation of relative
dominance. A focused pair review completes that pair's comparison. If the available
facts do not distinguish a cross, explain that specific comparison limit while
still giving the supported event/currency potential-impact verdicts. It does not
turn the researched currencies into "Review needed" or neutral/mixed verdicts.

## Dates, coverage and checkpoints

Follow [Thesis research](thesis-research.md) and the authoritative
[Thesis contract](../../../../docs/THESIS.md). Record original developments and
what reinforces, offsets or supersedes them; a new assessment time is not new
fundamental information. A retained verdict needs actual investigation of the
intervening period, not a reread of the old anchor. No event-count quota or
invented fresh evidence is permitted. Missing decisive evidence is explicit.

Old pair-relative rows remain historical context and require a new standalone
assessment before comparison. A change of basis is not a backdated market reversal.
