# Fundamental assessment

Use the parent research procedure. This reference owns interpretation and compact
answers; [Thesis research](thesis-research.md) owns interval-save workflow and
[the contract](../../../../docs/THESIS.md) owns storage/UI mechanics.

## Direction and dominant reason

For a researched pair lead with BUY or SELL, cutoff, horizon, dominant relative
mechanism and a concrete invalidation. STRONG BUY/SELL is supported qualitative
conviction, never a numerical score or a promise of candle size. Standalone
currencies use the [shared pressure scale](standalone-fundamentals.md), with
material opposition. Never populate a broad currency verdict from a single USD leg.
Compare both sides and check material cross-specific hazards; a same-direction
pair requires comparing pressure over fresh overlapping horizons.

The first task is simple: verified event → potential currency impact and intensity
→ economic reason. The user performs technical execution. A potential-impact
verdict does not require realised buying/selling, a live curve, agreement among
forecasters or proof of a profitable entry. Keep event impact and the combined
currency view distinct when their directions or horizons differ.

Weigh causal importance rather than counting releases. An inconclusive new
release need not erase a supported mechanism; missing new news is not itself a
reason to stay flat. Do not preserve a prior verdict automatically, force the
opposite call or use neutral/mixed language to avoid judgment when evidence
supports direction. Resolve known competing mechanisms into the best supported
potential verdict; do not treat the work of weighing them as an information gap.
Only a genuinely unverified decisive fact justifies withholding the claim that
depends on it. State supported impacts from the verified evidence in the meantime.

For a release separate incremental information from the overall currency/pair
bias and the user's execution decision. Keep surveys, official guidance and
market-implied paths separately attributed and timed. An expected hike with a
lower future path can weaken a currency; a less dovish cut can strengthen it.
Do not claim either mechanically or assert something was priced in without
contemporaneous evidence. Components and revisions form one release package.

Explain potential investor demand through the actual channel: duration/yields,
funding, hedging, trade exposure, fiscal credibility, supply, risk or haven demand.
Observed flows, reported explanations and inference are distinct. A rate gap,
inventory draw or chart movement does not establish a private investor intention.

## Release and retention check

Identify the underlying policy backdrop, the release's incremental effect against
attributed expectations, and the dominant pressure over the assessment's stated
horizon. They can differ. Explain why the chosen mechanism outweighs the strongest
verified opposition now; an old tightening story or a smaller intensity label is
not sufficient justification for retaining a direction after contrary guidance.
Change direction when the opposite mechanism dominates. Ordinary opposition
belongs in the reason and may change intensity; it does not require unanimity or
a "Review needed" result. If an essential actual or remark is genuinely unknown,
identify it precisely and limit only the unsupported claim.
An in-line release need not invalidate an independently supported continuing thesis.

When available, use time-attributed futures/OIS and policy-sensitive yields to
check the proposed transmission. Distinguish short-rate expectations from long
yields, which can move for other reasons. Reconcile a material contradiction or
disclose it; neither FX movement nor one yield supplies a mechanical verdict.
This check requires no candles and does not make live pricing a prerequisite for
a sourced fundamental interpretation. Compare like-for-like definitions and
capture times; different report snapshots do not establish measured repricing.
Read material contrary path/pricing reporting before announcing an upgrade, rather
than announcing the favourable forecast first and reconciling it afterwards.

Treat statement, opening remarks and Q&A as successive information sets. Deliver
the supported first stage promptly, then check available decisive later guidance
before finishing the review. Credible reporting can establish the material remarks
without a final official transcript. A still-pending material stage makes the
first verdict provisional through that stage; an elapsed unverified stage remains
a gap, not a completed review or an automatically renewed session verdict.
Dynamic article bodies may change: preserve the observed version/cutoff and never
attribute later text or market observations to an earlier assessment.

## Continuation, retracement or a changed thesis

For reversal/recovery/continuation questions give the immediate-session view and
next 1–5 trading sessions unless the user narrows the horizon. Check what happened
since the prior assessment: actuals, later guidance/Q&A, counterpart changes,
supply restoration, de-escalation and haven/funding demand. Later information can
outweigh the original surprise even when the original central bank has not moved.

State retained, strengthened, weakened or reversed against a comparable prior
view, with decisive evidence. A weaker buy case is not proof of SELL. When the
opposite mechanism now dominates, change direction promptly rather than keeping
it indefinitely as recovery risk. A change from pair-relative to standalone is a
new assessment basis, not evidence of a market reversal at a guessed minute.

A stale or invalidated entry is retired separately from the directional backdrop.
A new source retrieval or copied window does not reactivate it. Do not invent
profit-taking, short covering or a headline cause from the chart. Explicit price-
aware questions permit only bounded feedback under entry decisions; historical
measurement uses Review Historical Research.

## Events, sources and horizons

Resolve material just-elapsed and imminent stages before a long backdrop.
Decision, statement, projections, opening remarks and Q&A have separate clocks.
Check official schedules and actual publication, including overnight interviews;
an elapsed scheduled hour does not prove actuals arrived. Unknown/indicative
broadcast hours stay uncertain and do not supply a precise reminder.

Research the requested period plus relevant upcoming stages over seven days or
longer holding horizon. Include a later policy meeting outside that scan when
central to the mechanism. USD always considers Treasury auctions, buyback stages
and relevant Fed/NYFed operations; offered amounts are not accepted amounts.
Routine financing/implementation is not automatic QE or a directional FX signal.
For both currencies check material statistics, fiscal/trade/energy and unscheduled
risks beyond the broker filters. Open decisive sources; disclose failed access and
uncovered intervals. Do not convert a dynamic calendar shell into verified coverage.

## Compact delivery and saving

Give the current decision early, then supported LOOK HERE NEXT candidates when
relevant and chronological NEAR-TERM & UPCOMING EVENTS. Include only the dated
backdrop needed to explain the mechanism. Name expectations and conditional
outcomes for preparation; never present future results as known. A pending event
is a checkpoint, not a blanket prohibition on a supported current bias.

Requested broad Thesis research saves developments/assessments under the shared
workflow, with no compulsory entry section, same-day windows or news-item quota.
Focused live invocations may refresh affected existing live assessments without
a second save prompt; preserve unresearched currencies and explain decisive gaps.
Save original source clocks and research-as-of separately, then read back the
range and summary. Pair-journal saves retain their requested scope and legacy
contract. No trading, automatic archiving or continuous monitoring follows.
