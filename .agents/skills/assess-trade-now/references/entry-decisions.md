# From direction to an entry opportunity

Read for bare invocations, opportunity/entry requests and dated Thesis fills.
Real capital may be at stake. A plausible story is insufficient: identify the
active mechanism, its evidence, timing, material opposition and what defeats it.
Do not manufacture an entry to satisfy a format or the user's existing position.

## One invocation is enough

With no symbol or narrower task, `$assess-trade-now` means: find the strongest
supported opportunities now among the eight currencies and eligible commodities.
Also surface exceptional outside-universe candidates discovered incidentally
under [opportunity discovery](opportunity-discovery.md#exceptional-opportunities-outside-fx).
Use a brief catalyst sweep, then investigate material candidates and their real
alternatives. Default to the current trading session; when closed, distinguish
the next tradable session and reopening/gap risk from current execution. Verify
the instrument's session rather than assuming every market shares FX hours.
Add a next 1–5-session view only when requested or materially different.
Seven-day schedule coverage is not the direction's holding period.

Lead with at most three leading verified candidates, ordered by the clarity and
current relevance of their mechanism, not invented return estimates or scores.
Say which markets received focused comparison. A brief sweep does not prove a
market-wide winner. A named pair remains the main task; explicit scope-only,
fundamental-only, no-candles, read-only and prompt-only overrides take precedence.
Apply [standalone fundamentals](standalone-fundamentals.md), including comparable
Strong / Moderate / Slight pressure and explicit evidence dates. Complete relevant
same-direction comparisons; equal grades alone are not a completed cross review.
Thesis stores currency developments and assessments; cross explanations stay in
chat/reasons without recreating deleted pair journals.

## Manual technical workflow

The user chooses support/resistance, price alerts and execution. Default to
fundamentals only, including for an entry question; fetch price/candles only on
an explicit price-aware request. Timestamped yields, policy pricing and commodity
structure remain useful fundamental evidence. A supplied chart or price alert
does not require a technical audit, and touching a level does not validate or
refresh the fundamental verdict.

When a meaningful relative opportunity exists, name a leading verified pair or
cross, BUY/SELL, the mechanism on both sides, current-session horizon, concrete
invalidation and nearest material reassessment checkpoint. Include at most one
useful alternative in a release response. Consider non-USD crosses when their
relative mechanism is clearer; do not infer them by mechanically combining saved
currency labels whose comparisons or horizons differ. Check both currency stories
and the cross's own hazards. Explain why the leading candidate is preferable
among the candidates actually checked; an incomplete comparison is not a market-wide
ranking. No candidate quota or full 28-pair scan is required.

Separate the incremental release implication, overall fundamental direction and
the user's technical execution. An active fundamental opportunity means the
mechanism operates now, not that a chart entry has qualified. A price-alert
follow-up refreshes intervening news, invalidation and elapsed checkpoints before
retaining/reversing the call. Do not automatically wait for another catalyst,
redate underlying evidence, advance Checkpoint, arm an alarm or save Thesis merely because a
price alert fired. Requested Thesis saves retain the existing append/read-back
contract, with pair comparisons in chat rather than the daily currency rows.

## Decision card

Keep the answer compact; put depth and source coverage in saved evidence.

- **Instrument / BUY or SELL / horizon / cutoff.** Currency-only comparisons
  retain strengthening/weakening wording. Identify the relevant counterpart.
- **Status:** active opportunity (mechanism operating), conditional opportunity
  (name the unmet activation), or directional backdrop (supported bias without
  an established entry case). These are opportunity states, not neutral biases.
- **Prior expectation → new information or continuing pressure → remaining case.**
  Attribute surveys, official guidance and market-implied expectations separately
  with their observation times. A statement that something was priced in needs
  evidence. Missing pricing is a specific gap, not permission to invent it or
  ignore a supported supply, fiscal or haven mechanism. No fresh headline is
  necessary when an established mechanism remains active.
- **Potential buying/selling:** explain the economic incentive and instrument
  exposure; identify participants only when justified. Label the basis observed,
  reported (name the source), or inferred. A position change is not its motive;
  an inventory draw is not observed investor buying. Consider hedging, funding,
  duration, collateral and mandate constraints when they change the conclusion.
  Do not require private flow visibility to give a supported directional verdict.
- **Market feedback / activation / invalidation / next checkpoint.** Show the
  strongest verified competing mechanism when material. Differentiate an active
  fundamental case from an execution level, fill or measured risk/reward.
  On a reassessment, explicitly say whether the previous entry still qualifies,
  is conditional, or no longer qualifies, and give the decisive reason. A retained
  directional bias does not renew an invalidated entry. Keep a retired entry out
  of the current opportunity list; preserve its earlier saved version.

An entry case requires a mechanism relevant now, a reason pressure can persist
or expectations may adjust further, a tradable instrument with known session,
and a concrete falsifier. It need not have a scheduled catalyst or unanimous data.
If no candidate meets that standard, say so and retain supported directional
biases. Do not force a reverse verdict or fabricate a trade opportunity.

## Bounded market feedback

For this user's manual technical workflow, price feedback requires an explicit
price-aware request. When requested, entry-focused modes permit a small check.
Check timestamped policy pricing/relevant yields or commodity structure, and
the smallest useful completed price window where available. Start with saved
evidence; resolve exact symbol/provider, clock, session and forming bars. Use
the supported OHLC CLI, sequential captures, bounded requests and explicit gaps.
No technical-indicator inventory or broad history pull. Price extent alone
cannot prove crowding, exhaustion, order flow or a mispricing. Costs, spreads
and financing qualify entry suitability when verified; never invent them.

An explicit fundamental-only/no-candles override suppresses candle retrieval.
Broad fundamental research does not fetch eight price histories; feedback is limited
to shortlisted entry candidates. If feedback is unavailable, report that once
and narrow the entry claim; do not erase a supported fundamental direction.
Pure bias and release-first verdicts remain fundamental-first. Never delay an
imminent release's actuals/first verdict for prices. Full historical measurement
uses Review Historical Research; all simulated feedback stops at its as-of time.

## Evidence that can improve this workflow

When a thesis/opportunity save is requested, freeze cutoff, expectation baseline,
decision, status, evidence basis, horizon and falsifier before outcomes. Later
updates append versions and separate new facts from the earlier information set.
Compare prospective episodes, including losing and rejected candidates; record
costs/drawdown only when measured. A retrospective explanation, cherry-picked
rally or simulated fill does not establish profitability. No new monitoring,
automatic trade, account action or full Highlight journal write follows from a bare invocation. Focused live Thesis updates keep their standing permission.
