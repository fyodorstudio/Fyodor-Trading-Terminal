# Early opportunity discovery and market feedback

Read for fresh conversational assessments and opportunity/holding reviews.
The purpose is to direct attention while a mechanism is forming, then make the
directional decision when it dominates. A headline celebrating a large move is
not the required starting signal. This is an active-invocation workflow, not a
news subscription or continuous monitor.

## Discover beneficiaries before the retrospective headline

Alongside the requested pair, briefly sweep USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CHF, CAD, AUD and
NZD plus material Brent/WTI and gold catalysts for fresh policy/news catalysts and changing global conditions. Use dated
market reporting and official communications, including off-calendar developments.
Search the past 24 hours and nearer session developments; retain older active
context with its original date. Identify who benefits and who loses through
policy expectations, sovereign yields, funding, equity/credit stress, energy,
trade, geopolitics and intervention risk. An empty broker calendar is insufficient.

Keep this sweep small enough that it cannot delay imminent-release actuals or a
supported requested-pair verdict. Deep research stays focused on the request and
material discovered candidates. Respect explicit scope-only/no-extra-currencies,
prompt-only, no-web and no-save constraints. A narrow override stays narrow;
state that the opportunity sweep was excluded rather than claiming market-wide
coverage. A release countdown reuses preparation and refreshes exceptional news;
it does not start a new eight-currency investigation at publication.

For a material candidate beyond the named scope, verify its decisive sources and
both currency stories before promoting it. State which candidates received deep
research; a brief sweep is not a fully ranked market comparison. A separate source
coverage receipt can help an audit, but is not required to save verified findings
in Thesis or deliver a supported current decision. Never mislabel an event's
currency/instrument to satisfy a checklist. Relevant pins/reminders use the parent
identity/preflight/read-back procedure; not every research finding needs a pin.

## Test the current market regime

During risk aversion, investigate whether USD, JPY or CHF actually benefits and
why. Do not automatically assign every haven episode to USD or every equity
decline to JPY. Falling US yields, reduced Fed tightening expectations or stress
originating in US assets can support a JPY-over-USD mechanism. Japan's domestic
data need not improve for that relative opportunity to develop. Energy-import
costs, funding demand and the counterpart's policy path can oppose it; weigh the
active mechanism rather than count bullish and bearish headlines.

Distinguish rate levels from changes in expected paths. A possible BOJ hike months
ahead alone does not establish today's yen demand. Use dated reporting/pricing
to establish the active channel, and label causal inference. Do not assert carry
unwinding, repatriation or foreign capital inflows without evidence. A strong
bond auction alone is not proof of FX buying. One decisive verified development
can establish a candidate; multiple syndications are not independent confirmation.

## LOOK HERE NEXT

Surface up to three supported directional candidates near the top of the answer:

- **Currency and direction:** explicit attention cue, such as "TAKE A CLOSE LOOK
  AT JPY — UPSIDE CANDIDATE versus USD", and the relevant comparison.
- **Why now:** fresh development, original publication time/date and direct source.
- **Opportunity stage:** emerging, active, or already substantially underway.
  Establish the stage from timestamped evidence; if price extent was not checked,
  say so rather than pretending the move has not started or is exhausted.
- **Activation:** the development that would establish or reinforce the thesis.
  When already present, state it and give the supported directional verdict now.
- **Invalidation:** one concrete change that defeats the mechanism.
- **Next check:** the nearest material event or session checkpoint and what to
  reassess, with verified Jakarta time or unknown hour. This creates no timer.

An emerging candidate merits attention before it supports a full trade verdict.
Do not hold a dominant active opposite mechanism indefinitely at watch status.
Do not pad the list or call it the market's best opportunities without comparing
the relevant alternatives. No candidate is a legitimate sweep result when the
opened evidence establishes none; explain the scope checked briefly. If requested
output must remain short, compress each candidate to its essential attention cue,
why now and invalidation rather than expanding into another backdrop table.
Standalone attention cues do not establish strength against all seven currencies.
For JPYX or another named index, verify provider, constituents, weights and quote
direction before index-specific claims; USDJPY alone is not that basket.

## Bounded price-aware feedback

Pure bias/release-first assessments are fundamental-first. Entry-focused modes
use the fundamental-only manual technical workflow under [entry decisions](entry-decisions.md);
price feedback requires an explicit price-aware request. An explicit
price-aware request also permits its supplied chart or bounded price window. A simple attachment or claim that price rose supplies user context; it
does not silently override an explicit no-candles request. No browser/screenshot
UI audit is implied. Use the supplied artifact or terminal evidence when authorized.

Resolve symbol/provider, clock, horizon and whether the data is live, completed
or forming. Use the smallest useful saved or fetched window; bridge OHLC calls
remain sequential and bounded, with coverage and broker clock checks from
AGENTS.md. Do not fetch broad histories or add technical-indicator inventories.
A historical as-of price check must stop at the chosen cutoff; never inspect the
later rally while deciding what was knowable before it. Full release-reaction or
multi-session price measurement routes to Review Historical Research.

When price persistently contradicts the thesis over the requested horizon,
immediately investigate updated policy pricing, counterpart developments,
trading-partner news, liquidity and risk conditions. Explicitly decide whether
the thesis is retained, reversed or withdrawn for a decisive unresolved fact.
Do not dismiss the contradiction as noise, profit-taking or short covering
without evidence. Keep the observed move separate from its proposed explanation;
price feedback challenges a stale thesis without proving an unseen cause.
Entry levels, stops, sizing and execution remain the user's decisions.
