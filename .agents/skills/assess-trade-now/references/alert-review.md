# Upcoming alerts and holding/reassessment review

This mode is owned by Assess Trade Now. Run its immediate discovery and pin pass
first; do not ask the user to invoke a separate skill. A request for alerts alone
needs a concise event shortlist, not an unsolicited full pair assessment.

## Scope and relevance

Use the requested pair/currencies and horizon. A pair's holding/exit or next
opportunity question scans at least seven calendar days for both currencies,
extending through a longer holding horizon. An unspecified broad shortlist scans
all eight currencies for 90 days, emphasizing the next few weeks; later events
are provisional and need a fresh expectations/backdrop check near publication.
Only the broad shortlist defaults to 90 days/all currencies.

Refresh the latest policy stance, conditional guidance, expected paths and active
off-calendar mechanisms from the web. Identify what new information can change
the current concern: inflation/pass-through, wages/employment, demand, financing,
fiscal credibility, energy or geopolitical conditions. A forecast, central-bank
projection and identified market pricing are distinct evidence. Distinguish
flash/first estimates from later finals and new information from repeated remarks.

Include material market-moving operations and liquidity/issuance stages even
without an established directional outcome. They may disrupt an intraday setup
while leaving the broader thesis intact. Explain which channel matters now;
do not classify a routine operation as a policy-rate change or QE. Give supported
conditional outcomes, not a promised candle, entry, probability or return.

Check central-bank speaking/media programmes and conference agendas, including
overnight Q&A. Attendance does not establish policy guidance. Respect quiet
periods and explicit exclusions; an event excluding policy can still matter
through financial stability. Capture an exact official event/day stream where
available; distinguish live-only access, an expected publication and retrievable
text. A scheduled stream does not prove broadcasting, and an interview time need
not be broadcast time. Preserve approximate windows/date-only schedules.

## Source identity, pins and handoff

Use the parent skill's mandatory `highlights`, `limitations`, separate `external`
and complete scoped `upcoming` reads, with pagination. Read focused prior journals
only when they add useful context. Exact broker source/value IDs distinguish
occurrences; a label alone does not. Unknown IDs remain admissible Other events.
Web schedules/actuals take precedence over flawed broker fields; preserve the
raw feed and append a limitation and sourced official stage when mismatched.

Under the parent skill's standing authorization, pin/restore verified material
occurrences as found and request chart icons for dated External Source records.
Reuse active pins/stable keys and preserve histories. A source not yet retrieved
is a watch/retrieval gap, not a confirmed timed event. Do not manufacture a broker
ID or a precise countdown. Read back every write and complete the coverage receipt.

If the user explicitly requests AI impact labels, use
[Alert impact](../../../../docs/HIGHLIGHT-JOURNAL.md#ai-impact-in-alert).
Read the prior rating and save the requested High/Medium/Low with occurrence-specific
reason, sources and real cutoff. No fixed indicator tiers, retrospective candle
size or MT5 importance fallback; these labels are separate from direction/entry
quality. A request to fill Highlight covers every active broker and external pin,
with complete pagination and live route read-back, not just the leading shortlist.

An explicit prompt-only/deferred request reuses the accepted shortlist and focused
IDs without new macro or price research. Save separate preparation and after-release
templates with `prompt save` when requested; leave Before research pending.
Preserve known gaps with `limitations append`. The user can request fresh same-day
preparation and later interpretation; templates alone do not establish a verdict.

## Deliver the review

Lead with the nearest material stage, including elapsed publications whose actuals
remain unverified. Then give a short chronological list: event, Jakarta AM/PM time
(or unconfirmed hour), source, present relevance and outcome that supports,
challenges or invalidates the thesis. Separate timed stages from unscheduled
triggers. Note whether each is newly pinned, already present, restored, saved
outside the broker feed, read-only at the user's request or unresolved.

For holding questions, explain a material reassessment/closing consideration
without prescribing closure before every release. For next-opportunity questions,
keep the supported current bias separate from the absence of a new catalyst.
Suggested return times do not create alarms or coverage. State the checked window
and specific unresolved source gaps; incomplete coverage cannot support an all-clear.

Do not fetch or analyze candles/OHLC, including saved evidence. Keep chart levels,
technical entries, stops, sizing and trade execution with the user. Full journals
and Thesis writes still need their own request; no service restarts or monitoring.
