# Thesis research and interval updates

Use this workflow whenever the user asks to research a period and fill/update
Thesis. The user need only name the period/currencies or invoke Assess Trade Now.
The [Thesis contract](../../../../docs/THESIS.md) owns the JSON, CLI, UI and clocks;
do not reproduce those schemas or a giant copied prompt here.

1. Read `thesis research --scope ALL --from <date> --to <date>` and the live summary.
   For one currency use `--scope GBP` (or the requested currency) to avoid loading
   unrelated bodies; the response still includes its cited older foundations.
   Research all eight for a broad invocation, or the requested affected currencies
   for a focused refresh. Carrying a row is not assessing it.
2. Research the interval broadly, including policy communications, economic data
   and material off-calendar developments. Fill intervening gaps since the previous
   assessment. Open decisive sources and actively seek offsets/contrary news.
   There is no item quota; several components/syndications are one release package.
3. Store distinct developments with stable keys, factual summaries, original source
   dates/hours, sources and currency-specific implication/thesis effect. One event
   can affect several currencies differently. Link existing raw calendar/external
   identities when useful, without copying raw observations into research.
4. Reuse existing developments. A changed fact or interpretation appends an explained
   version; the tool resolves/fixes exact evidence versions for the assessment.
   Keep still-active evidence before the interval in `background_keys`, not repeated
   as today's news. Do not claim a later interpretation was made at event time.
5. Save a broad or focused assessment: standalone qualitative pressure, dominant
   remaining reason, material offsets, horizon and next relevant checkpoint. Record
   each material directional event's potential strengthening/weakening impact and
   intensity in its implication. Give a researched potential verdict despite
   ordinary uncertainty or known opposition. Findings alone and a missing-fact
   gap remain available for genuinely unverified decisive information, not as a
   substitute for completing the requested judgment.
   Declare the reviewed stage as complete or provisional under the Thesis
   contract. If an elapsed material stage could overturn the old call but its
   decisive contents are still unverified, save the specific gap without a fresh
   direction/assessment clock; retain the old call as previous evidence. If new
   stage evidence supports an assessment, state its provisional status explicitly.
6. Append through `thesis research-append`, then read back the range and live summary.
   Explain retained/changed pressure and any unresolved intervals/access gaps.

## Efficient refreshes

Start from each requested currency's last completed coverage, unresolved gaps,
active evidence and elapsed/next checkpoint. The header's latest time is not a
coverage cutoff for an unresearched row. Reuse loaded instructions and focused
saved context; do not repeatedly read whole ledgers, manuals or source inventories.
Retrieve the selected currency's active foundations and their sources, not just
the newest anchor or visible day's rows. The UI's earlier snapshots are a viewing
archive, not evidence that the agent has checked policy relevance or complete
coverage. Apply the parent's causal weighting before retaining/reversing a view.
Use focused currency/date fields from range responses rather than dumping complete
ledgers or repeatedly serializing unchanged history. Reuse existing fetch/save/
validator tooling; do not author bespoke audit scripts for each routine refresh.

Check fresh dated news and source/calendar changes across the requested scope,
including contrary and off-calendar developments. A broad refresh still assesses
all eight and covers the parent's source families. Concentrate deeper retrieval
on new facts, changed expectations, missing periods and challenged mechanisms.
Unchanged official foundations can remain dated background; neither a reread nor
an empty broker calendar establishes that nothing changed. Widen research when
coverage is stale/incomplete, material contradictions emerge or the user requests it.
If a pass runs long, make a focused final check for material news arriving since
the initial sweep. Resolve relevant new facts before retaining a verdict, without
restarting unchanged background research or claiming continuous monitoring.

Use known direct routes first, batch independent retrievals and read a shared
release once. Apply it separately to each affected currency; syndications and
release components are not extra votes. Keep raw snapshots when useful, but return
focused fields/passages and coverage gaps instead of dumping complete responses.
Reuse verified schedules within their horizon while checking changed times and
resolving elapsed stages. A normal refresh does not require a new 90-day inventory,
coverage receipt or pin/alarm audit unless that work is requested or materially needed.

Stop a failed route after a suitable fallback unless a distinct decisive question
justifies more research. Deliver a supported stage-specific decision promptly;
continue remaining requested coverage, the save and left-panel verification in the
same turn. Do not label that early decision a completed broad assessment. Once
those checks pass, finish without repeating them absent a new failure or change.
One read-back of the saved range and summary plus one frontend-validator check
through either running summary route is sufficient in an unchanged runtime.
Additional endpoint comparisons, full suites, builds or service restarts need a
specific failure or implementation change. When diagnosing slow work, distinguish
available retrieval, first-verdict and save/verification times; do not invent a
latency breakdown or impose a speed target that skips decisive/contrary evidence.

## Left-panel freshness audit

The left **Fundamentals** panel is the user's primary monitoring view. Every
authorized live research save includes checking its projection, even when the
user did not separately request a left-panel update. Save through the ledger;
the read-only panel derives its rows automatically. Do not edit labels directly
or create a duplicate verdict file.

Read `pnpm analyst thesis summary --scope ALL` before research and after saving.
Verify each affected currency's standalone verdict/intensity, assessment clock,
original evidence anchor and next checkpoint or explicit Review needed gap.
A focused refresh must preserve the other currencies and their clocks. When a
gap retains an older verdict, its supporting evidence anchor must also remain
the one assessed then; newer unassessed findings stay in Thesis as pending review.

Also check the running `http://127.0.0.1:8002/api/v1/theses/summary` endpoint, or
the UI proxy `/storage-api/theses/summary`, when available. Verify its response
against the frontend summary validator with terminal-based tooling; CLI/HTTP
success alone does not establish that the panel accepts the response. Resolve a
local runtime/contract error within authorized scope or report the exact delivery
gap. Do not claim the left panel updated while its summary is unavailable or
invalid. Leave visual acceptance to the user; no browser automation is needed.
Check that declared provisional status survives the ledger, range read and summary
projection. Expired checkpoints and decisive gaps must visibly qualify retained
verdicts as **Previous assessment**; a future unresolved stage says **Provisional**.
Schema acceptance and persistence prove delivery, not financial accuracy or source
coverage. Complete the parent's decision check separately.

The static Jakarta **Last Assessed** header means the latest completed, saved live
assessment/research pass, including explicitly recorded gaps. Set its research
cutoff when completing the pass; an initial scan, reload or save alone does not
complete research. A read-only pass cannot update the saved panel. Row assessment
clocks and gaps preserve the scope of focused work; the header does not imply
every currency received a new supported direction.

Evidence age is the latest significant dated development referenced by that
assessment, not the age of the research. After checking the intervening period,
an unchanged supported thesis may have a fresh assessment and an older anchor.
Do not manufacture a newer anchor, rewrite publication dates, force direction or
mark Review needed solely because no newer significant event exists. Use Review
needed only for a genuinely unavailable decisive fact, naming what is missing and
which claim depends on it. Known conflicting mechanisms, missing live OIS, routine
opposition or failure to choose a grade do not qualify. Resolve those during the
invocation and save the supported potential verdict. The timeline retains all
supporting/contrary dates; polling, saves and retrievals never make old news new.

A full requested refresh checks all eight. A focused release refresh resolves the
relevant actuals/guidance and affected/decisively overdue mechanisms without
starting unrelated investigations. Preserve unresearched rows and their clocks.
A checkpoint is a review deadline, not a promise of direction until then; resolve
elapsed stages before selecting a new verified stage. Shared global deadlines need
a concrete channel for each affected currency. Date-only schedules get no invented
hour or precise bell. A missing schedule hour limits timer precision; it does not
prevent a potential-impact verdict. Never use Review needed as the normal result
of a user asking the agent to review known evidence.

Keep live and historical information sets separate. A historical period uses
labelled reconstruction and cannot replace live verdicts. Future days stay unfilled.
The dock selects one currency/date and its archive preserves earlier currency
snapshots. Saved pair journals were deleted at the user’s request; do not recreate
them without an explicit request. New findings use the research ledger rather
than rebuilding daily blobs. Entry comparisons
are optional outputs. Saving research requires neither a pin nor a reminder for
every row; significant reminders use the separate alert procedure.
