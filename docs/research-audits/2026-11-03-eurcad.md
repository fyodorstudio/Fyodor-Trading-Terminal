pre-simplification workflow. Its controls are now retired; the original files and
records remain in [the preservation archive](../RESEARCH-ARCHIVE.md). Current news
uses [short research rules](../RESEARCH-RULES.md) and scoped reviewed lessons.

and funding support was repeatedly described as an offset without establishing

The error was in comparative weighting and renewal. Oil relief can help the euro
area's import bill and hurt Canadian export income, but that channel alone did
not establish its dominance over CAD's trade/funding support and EUR's own policy
and financing opposition. Demanding a signed Canadian trade deal while crediting
expected relief elsewhere applied an asymmetric standard. A US–Canada spread is
also not a measured EUR–Canada spread. Those limitations must change the decision
when they remove its support, rather than merely decorate its caveats.

across three distinct currency episodes, plus a checkpoint failure. Revisions of

| Failure | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Opposing evidence named but dismissed without comparison | Evidence-linked bull/bear cases over the same horizon, expectation baseline, incremental/continuing mechanism and transmission; exact selected source versions. Declared conclusion must match the issued judgment. |
| Prior premise renewed by habit | Exact latest prior-call identity, explicit survives/replaced judgment, full intervening coverage, and the latest scoped reviewed process failure plus a substantive response. |
| Grade compressed by generic uncertainty | Explicit Strong/Moderate/Slight causal comparison matching the selected intensity, for standalone currencies and graded pairs. Strong remains allowed with material but subordinate opposition. |
| A fresh save clock hides old investigation | Actual source-check clocks and findings; cutoff equals last real check. Original evidence dates remain unchanged. Missing legacy review/check data stays visibly unknown. |
| Checkpoint moved without research | Original elapsed stage must be resolved with selected post-stage evidence and a matching check; a call already expired at delivery is rejected. |
| Currency labels automatically imply a cross or D1 colour | Independent pair comparison and independent open-to-close D1 review, each with its own horizon and prior version. Withdrawal of BUY does not mechanically create SELL. |
| A preflight receipt outlives an intervening change | Read-only preflight, then repeat the same admission checks against the current ledger under the append lock. |
| Upcoming catalyst hidden in pair detail | Recommended Pairs exposes the saved currency-specific catalyst stages, Jakarta deadline/countdown, sources and expired state, alongside original evidence age and recorded news coverage. |
