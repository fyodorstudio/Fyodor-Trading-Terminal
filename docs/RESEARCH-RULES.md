# Research rules

Read this short file for live news refreshes. Retrieve focused lessons with
`pnpm analyst news lessons --scope <currency>` when the mechanism or a repeated
failure is relevant; do not load raw archives on every invocation.

- Open decisive source bodies. Search snippets, inaccessible articles and a new
  retrieval timestamp do not verify new actuals or a story's publication time.
  Prefer official releases for numbers and policy, original reporting for news.
  If a route fails, try a suitable alternative and disclose the remaining gap.
- Keep publication, event, source-check and save clocks distinct. Use Jakarta
  AM/PM in presentation and offset-aware instants in storage. A newly edited
  article containing old facts is not automatically a fresh development. Preserve
  unknown hours and original release vintages; never invent precise timing.
- Explain the causal implication, not just the headline's adjective. Compare
  actuals/guidance with attributed expectations and the bank's relevant concerns.
  Distinguish an expected hike from a new change in the expected path. Components
  of one release and syndicated copies are not independent votes.
- Check the strongest material contrary news. Apply the same evidence standard
  to both mechanisms. If opposition removes the support for a conclusion, change
  the conclusion; do not retain it merely by adding a caveat. A previous judgment
  is context, not a reason to renew it.
- News implication is conditional currency pressure, not proof of realised
  flows, price direction, a profitable entry or strength against every peer.
  Permit mixed/no clear fresh signal when the evidence warrants it. Research both
  currencies before a specifically requested pair comparison. Do not derive a
  pair recommendation or broker D1 colour from a standalone news label.
- Separate the three-hour fresh window from older active background. Quiet
  coverage is a valid result. A failed search is not proof that nothing happened.
  Never copy an unresearched currency merely to make all rows look current.
- Keep facts, attribution and inference distinguishable. No invented forecasts,
  funding flows, calibration, numerical probabilities, returns or technical levels.
  Use normal spaced prose: `JPY 330 bn`, `128 bp`, `November 4`.
- Historical favourable extremes do not prove a correct closing verdict or
  executable profit. Later facts cannot repair an earlier call. Full failure
  records remain archived; automatic scoring and outcome capture are retired.

No trades, broker/account changes, automatic AI monitoring, reminder changes or
current technical scans are part of a news refresh. A separately requested
historical investigation may use the existing bounded candle tools.
