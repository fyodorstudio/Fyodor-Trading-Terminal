# Research rules

For requested financial research, read decisive source bodies and keep facts
separate from inference. Prefer original publishers. Do not invent actuals,
forecasts, probabilities or publication times. Check material contrary evidence.

Currency News follows the [News contract](CURRENCY-NEWS.md). Research only the
requested currencies; retain publication dates, independent clocks and originals.
Fundamentals contains facts; Implication contains agent conclusions. Historical
cutoffs still apply. Relevant lessons: `pnpm analyst news lessons --scope <currency>`.

Assess Trade Now includes its [global risk workflow](GLOBAL-RISK-WORKFLOW.md).
Preserve user reminder choices; research saves do not themselves change alarms.
