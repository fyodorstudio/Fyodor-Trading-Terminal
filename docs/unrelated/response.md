## 1. Major fundamental drivers

Binance $6.3B security breach — Strongly bearish

Reuters reported that Binance suffered a security breach affecting approximately $6.3 billion in crypto assets. Around $3 billion had been located or frozen. This creates substantial uncertainty about exchange liquidity and investor confidence. Importantly, this was an exchange breach, not a confirmed compromise of Bitcoin's blockchain.&#x20;

For a near-term bullish shift, I'd look for exchange operations stabilizing, fresh ETF inflows after the incident, and BTC reclaiming $80,000 sustainably. A break below $76,400 would strengthen the bearish case.
